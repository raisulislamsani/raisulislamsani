Our Story

The Burger
House Story

Born out of a simple passion for honest, mouth-watering food, The Burger House started as a small pop-up in Dhaka. We believed this community deserved better than frozen patties and soggy buns.

Today, we stand by our founding principles: meat ground fresh daily, buns baked by local artisans, and a commitment to creating an unforgettable culinary experience for everyone who walks through our doors.
